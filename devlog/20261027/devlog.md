# Investors of SPLORR!! (devlog, October 27, 2026)

**Made with AI.** A human and Claude Code made this together for 1-Bit Jam 15. The human was product owner and tester; Claude wrote the code, the tests, the sound and the words, and drew some of the art.

The theme was "Everything Has a Price". The idea: a dungeon where a shopkeeper charges for everything but never says "price", "cost" or "fee". Every charge is an investment or an opportunity, with the amount printed plainly. The first two rooms are quiet and free. The exit costs 50 coins and the dungeon only pays 40, so the last opportunity is **Peace of Mind**, which you had in the quiet rooms all along.

- Turn-based: one key press, one turn. Swinging your sword costs a coin.
- A scripted player proves the dungeon can be finished and that the exit stays out of reach.
- Art: 1-Bit Kingdom by Vryell, plus five sprites by Claude. Font: DINOBYTE by mby.

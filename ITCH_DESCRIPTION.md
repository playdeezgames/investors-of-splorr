**Investors of SPLORR!!**

You are a hero in a small dungeon, and the dungeon is a business. A shopkeeper meets you with opportunities: a torch, a mend, a word of advice. A door is an opportunity. So is a swing of your sword. Coins come from slimes and gems, never quite as fast as the opportunities do.

Seven rooms. The first two are quiet. Stay as long as you like.

Everything has a price, and the shopkeeper will never once call it that.

**Controls:** arrow keys or WASD to move, Enter or Z to confirm (and to stand still and rest), Esc or X to back out. One key press is one turn, so take your time. On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen d-pad and buttons.

**Made with:** Odin, compiled to WebAssembly, drawing to a plain 2D canvas. Two colours, as the jam asks.
